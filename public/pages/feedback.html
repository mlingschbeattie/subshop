If the app logs raw user input without redacting PII, sensitive data — credit card numbers, SSNs, passwords — accumulates silently in the log table. Anyone with log access sees everything.

      
Think of it like: A security camera recording everything including people's PIN entries at the ATM.

      
Real world: 2017 Uber breach — logs contained unredacted credentials and PII for 57 million users.
