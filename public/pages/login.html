When tokens are stored client-side and not validated server-side, users can forge their own identity. The app trusts whatever is in localStorage — change it, and you change who you are.

      
Think of it like: A bouncer who accepts any ID as long as it has the right format — he never actually calls to verify.

      
Real world: 2012 LinkedIn breach — 6.5M unsalted SHA-1 password hashes cracked within hours.
