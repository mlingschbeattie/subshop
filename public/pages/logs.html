If the app logs raw user input without redacting PII, sensitive data — credit card numbers, SSNs, passwords — accumulates silently in the log table. Anyone with access to the logs sees everything.

Think of it like: A security camera that records everything including people's PIN entries at the ATM.

Real world: 2017 Uber breach — logs contained unredacted credentials and PII for 57 million users and drivers.
