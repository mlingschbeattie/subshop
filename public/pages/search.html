🦆

When user input is placed directly into HTML without sanitization, it can become executable code. The browser can't tell the difference between your content and a script tag.

Think of it like: Passing someone a note that turns into a loudspeaker announcement — your words literally get executed by the system.

Real world: 2005 Samy worm — a MySpace XSS spread to 1 million users in 20 hours.
